About us

A youth-led group, rooted in Segel.

Segel Youths Association was formed by young people from Segel who wanted a structured way to work on the challenges and opportunities facing their community — together, rather than alone. The group is registered with the Kenyan government as a community-based organisation.

Marsabit County is a vast, largely pastoralist and semi-arid part of northern Kenya, and Segel is a rural village within it — rural enough that opportunity has to be built locally, which is exactly what this group exists to do.
